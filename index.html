What we’re about

Made for shared worlds.

Project Reimagined makes thoughtful Minecraft projects for people who play together. We focus on useful ideas that feel natural in-game, from expressive server-side particles to new ways to share a world.

Everything starts with the same goal: make multiplayer more joyful, more accessible, and more yours.
